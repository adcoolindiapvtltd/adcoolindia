Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how AdCool India Private Limited handles information when you browse adcoolindia.com or contact us about Panel Air Conditioners, Chillers or AMC services.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, company, phone number, email address, product or service interest, quantity, location and message. Please do not include sensitive personal information in the form.

How enquiries are processed

The enquiry forms are submitted to FormSubmit, which processes the form and forwards it to sales@adcoolindia.com. FormSubmit is an independent service and its own privacy terms apply to its processing. We use enquiry details to respond, prepare quotations, provide requested support and maintain necessary business records.

Storage and retention

Enquiry messages may remain in our business email and related business records for as long as needed to respond, provide services and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Contact us if you need to request access, correction or deletion; some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

Cookies, local storage and analytics

This site currently has no analytics provider configured and does not load optional analytics tracking. It uses browser local storage only to remember your cookie-notice or analytics-consent preference. If optional analytics are configured in the future, they will remain off unless you explicitly accept them. You can reopen Cookie settings from the site footer.

External services and links

Submitting a form sends information to FormSubmit. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp, which is governed by its own privacy terms. We do not control the data practices of these independent services.

Contact and privacy requests

For questions or requests about your information, contact AdCool India Private Limited at info@adcoolindia.com or call +91 99531 95901. Address: Tajnagar, Gurugram, Haryana, India.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.
