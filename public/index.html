Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Panel Air Conditioner?

A Panel Air Conditioner is a cooling unit used to help control the temperature inside electrical panels, automation cabinets, control panels and industrial enclosures.

What information is needed for a Chiller quote?

Share your cooling load, process details, operating conditions and site location. Our team will review the requirement and confirm suitable equipment and specifications.

What does an AMC cover?

An AMC can include scheduled inspections, preventive maintenance and performance checks for the covered Panel AC and Chiller equipment. The service scope is confirmed before work begins.

How can I request a quotation from AdCool?

Use the enquiry form and include the product or service required, location and requirement details. The AdCool team can then review your enquiry.

What is the difference between a Panel Air Conditioner and an industrial chiller?

A Panel Air Conditioner cools air inside an electrical enclosure. An industrial chiller provides cooling for a process, machine or equipment application. The right option depends on what needs to be cooled.
