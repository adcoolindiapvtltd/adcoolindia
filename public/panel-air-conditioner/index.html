Panel Air Conditioner FAQs

What does a Panel Air Conditioner cool?

A Panel Air Conditioner cools the interior of an electrical panel, control cabinet or industrial enclosure to help manage heat from enclosed equipment.

What information is needed to select a Panel Air Conditioner?

Share the panel dimensions, estimated heat load, power supply and site conditions. These details help the team review a suitable option and confirm its specifications.

No. Model specifications and pricing depend on the application and selected equipment. Share your panel and site requirements to request a suitable option and quotation.
