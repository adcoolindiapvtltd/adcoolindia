Industrial Chiller FAQs

How do I select an industrial chiller?

Chiller selection depends on the cooling load, process, required fluid and temperature range, operating conditions and site. Share these details so the team can review a suitable configuration.

What information is needed for an industrial chiller quote?

Provide the required cooling capacity or heat load, process and equipment being cooled, fluid and temperature range, operating schedule, site conditions, installation location and project timeline.

Are chiller capacity, configuration and price fixed?

No. Capacity, configuration, utilities, technical specifications and pricing depend on the application. These details are reviewed and confirmed before a quotation is prepared.

Can I request maintenance for an industrial chiller?

You can enquire about an Annual Maintenance Contract for installed chiller equipment. Applicability, coverage and service scope are reviewed and agreed for the equipment and site.
