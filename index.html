Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Panel Air Conditioner?

A Panel Air Conditioner is a cooling unit used to help control the temperature inside electrical panels, automation cabinets, control panels and industrial enclosures.

What is the difference between Air Cooled and Water Cooled Chillers?

Air cooled chillers reject heat to surrounding air through a condenser, while water cooled chillers transfer heat through a water-based condenser system. The suitable approach depends on the application and site conditions.

What does an HVAC AMC cover?

An AMC can include preventive maintenance, inspection, routine maintenance, troubleshooting, performance checks and support for covered HVAC equipment. Scope should be confirmed for your equipment.

How can I request a quotation from AdCool?

Use the enquiry form and include the product or service required, location and requirement details. The AdCool team can then review your enquiry.
